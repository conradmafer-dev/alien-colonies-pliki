Your second colony in Alien Colonies, step by step

Alien Colonies guide · Updated: 13 October 2026

A second planet gives you another source of resources and room to grow. First, you need a scouted destination, a colonizer and enough supplies to keep both colonies working after the expedition.

In this guide

  1. What do you need to colonize a planet?
  2. How to choose your second planet
  3. How to send a colonizer in the browser
  4. An example cargo calculation
  5. What happens on arrival?

What do you need to colonize a planet?

New empires start without a fleet, so you must build a colonizer before expanding. Each race has its own version: the Zetan World Seeder, Symbiont Colony Mother or Automaton Constructor. Check the ships stationed at your selected colony: a ship already on a mission is not available for another launch.

The destination must be an unoccupied planet known to your empire through scouting or observation. Knowing where an unexplored system is located is not enough. The colony limit is 12, including colonization missions already on their way. You can have up to 8 active missions, so a full mission list can also prevent a launch.

Choose your race before expanding. Read the race comparison if you are considering differences in production and ships. Do not assume the same expedition costs the same for every empire.

How to choose your second planet

Start with a destination that supports your economy. Metallic planets favor metal, crystalline planets favor crystals and icy planets favor fuel. Better production of one resource can mean worse production of another. Where information is available, compare building slots and environmental traits too.

Distance matters for both the first journey and later supply runs. Travel to another galaxy is much more expensive than a local route. A nearby planet with useful resources may be easier to develop than a distant one with a better multiplier for a single resource.

Check the target's status in Galaxy. Scout an unexplored system, and scout again if your information is old. Learn more about the age of your intelligence in the scouting report guide.

How to send a colonizer in the browser

  1. Select the colony where your colonizer and expedition supplies are located.
  2. Open Galaxy, find an unoccupied planet and choose the mission action.
  3. Select a colonization mission and at least one colonizer. Check every ship quantity.
  4. Enter the metal, crystals and fuel you want to deliver. You can include a cargo ship.
  5. Check the route and cost. Review travel time, cargo capacity and fuel consumption.
  6. Send the fleet after checking the plan. Previewing a route does not launch the mission.

The quoted fuel cost covers the return journey as well as the outward journey. Travel fuel is deducted in addition to any fuel carried as cargo. Your combined resource cargo must fit within the selected ships' capacity, and all supplies must be in the starting colony.

An example cargo calculation

Suppose you want to deliver 200 metal, 150 crystals and 50 fuel. That is 400 units of cargo. Check that your fleet has at least 400 cargo capacity. If the route quote were 80 fuel, you would need 130 fuel to launch: 50 in the hold and 80 for the journey.

This is an example calculation, not a fixed colonization price or a recommended starting stockpile for every player. The game calculates the actual cost and time for your route, race and fleet. Adding a ship may increase cargo capacity, but can also increase fuel consumption or travel time.

Check what will remain in your first colony. Keep enough for your next project and a later supply run. Resources on different planets do not form one shared stockpile that automatically pays for construction anywhere.

What happens on arrival?

The planet must still be unoccupied when the fleet arrives. If another player claims it first, or you no longer meet the colony limit, the expedition returns. A successfully submitted order does not guarantee a colony: read the mission's final report.

Successful colonization consumes one colonizer to establish the base. The other ships and delivered resources stay on the new planet. The colony receives basic economic buildings and starting supplies; it is not a copy of your developed home planet.

Select the new colony using the selector at the top of the game. Check energy, production, free building slots and the cost of your first upgrade. A cargo ship sent with the expedition will now be stationed there. Plan further deliveries using the fleet and mission guide, and use the economy guide to plan development.
