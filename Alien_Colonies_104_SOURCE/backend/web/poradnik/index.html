Alien Colonies guide · Updated: 13 October 2026

A strong colony starts with a plan. Learn how to balance mining, energy, research and fleets so your empire has the resources for its next move.

Alien Colonies is a space strategy game where you develop planets, choose a race and send fleets to new worlds. More ships are not the answer to every situation: you need to scout your target, calculate fuel costs and maintain your economy. These guides take you from your first decisions to working with other players.

How to use these guides

If you are starting an empire, begin with your first colony and choosing a race. Read the economy guide when you run short of a resource or energy. Before sending a colonizer, follow the second-colony guide; before attacking, read the scouting and fleet guides.

The guides explain the rules. Check your colony's catalog for exact costs, project times and requirements: these depend on your race, building levels, research and planet traits. A target's situation can also change after you scout it.

What matters in a multiplayer space strategy game?

Your colony's growth is tied to the wider galaxy. Another planet offers more mining opportunities, but needs investment and transport. An alliance lets you plan together, but does not replace scouting. Decide what a mission should achieve, then choose your fleet and check that your economy can afford it.
